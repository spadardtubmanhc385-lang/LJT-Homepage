I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the **HKUST NLP Group** at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. I received my **B.Eng.** degree from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024. Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. My research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
